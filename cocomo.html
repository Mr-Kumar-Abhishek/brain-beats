2. COCOMO Mathematical Formulations & Assumptions

Initial Development Equations

Organic:
\( \text{Effort} = 2.4 \times (\text{KLOC})^{1.05} \)
\( \text{TDEV} = 2.5 \times (\text{Effort})^{0.38} \)

Semi-Detached:
\( \text{Effort} = 3.0 \times (\text{KLOC})^{1.12} \)
\( \text{TDEV} = 2.5 \times (\text{Effort})^{0.35} \)

Embedded:
\( \text{Effort} = 3.6 \times (\text{KLOC})^{1.20} \)
\( \text{TDEV} = 2.5 \times (\text{Effort})^{0.32} \)

Software Maintenance Equations

Annual Change Traffic (ACT):
\( \text{ACT} = \frac{\text{SLOC}_{\text{added}} + \text{SLOC}_{\text{modified}}}{\text{SLOC}_{\text{total}}} \)

Annual Maintenance Effort (AME):
\( \text{AME} = \text{ACT} \times \text{Effort}_{\text{dev}} \quad [\text{PM/year}] \)

Annual Maintenance Cost (AMC):
\( \text{AMC} = \text{AME} \times \text{Labor Rate} \quad [\$/\text{year}] \)

5-Year Total Cost of Ownership (TCO):
\( \text{TCO}_{5\text{yr}} = \text{Cost}_{\text{dev}} + 5 \times \text{AMC} \)

Baseline parameters: Scale = 469.816 KLOC; Labor Rate = $8,000 USD / person-month ($96,000 / year fully burdened base).
