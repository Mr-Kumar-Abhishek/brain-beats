2. COCOMO Mathematical Formulations & Assumptions

Initial Development Equations

Organic:
\( \text{Effort} = 2.4 \times (\text{KLOC})^{1.05} \quad [\text{Person-Months}] \)
\( \text{TDEV} = 2.5 \times (\text{Effort})^{0.38} \quad [\text{Months}] \)

Semi-Detached:
\( \text{Effort} = 3.0 \times (\text{KLOC})^{1.12} \quad [\text{Person-Months}] \)
\( \text{TDEV} = 2.5 \times (\text{Effort})^{0.35} \quad [\text{Months}] \)

Embedded:
\( \text{Effort} = 3.6 \times (\text{KLOC})^{1.20} \quad [\text{Person-Months}] \)
\( \text{TDEV} = 2.5 \times (\text{Effort})^{0.32} \quad [\text{Months}] \)

Software Maintenance Equations

Annual Change Traffic (ACT):
\( \text{ACT} = \dfrac{\text{SLOC}_{\text{added}} + \text{SLOC}_{\text{modified}}}{\text{SLOC}_{\text{total}}} \)

Annual Maintenance Effort (AME):
\( \text{AME} = \text{ACT} \times \text{Effort}_{\text{dev}} \quad [\text{PM/year}] \)

Annual Maintenance Cost (AMC):
\( \text{AMC} = \text{AME} \times \text{Labor Rate} \quad [\text{USD/year}] \)

5-Year Total Cost of Ownership (TCO):
\( \text{TCO}_{5\text{yr}} = \text{Cost}_{\text{dev}} + 5 \times \text{AMC} \)

Baseline parameters: Scale = 533.963 KLOC; Labor Rate = $8,000 USD / person-month ($96,000 / year fully burdened base).

Effort Adjustment Factor (EAF) Formulation & Valuation

In the COCOMO 81 Intermediate model, the Effort Adjustment Factor (EAF) is computed as the composite product of all 15 cost driver attribute ratings:

\[ \begin{aligned} \text{EAF} &= \prod_{i=1}^{15} \text{Cost Driver}_i \\ &= 1.15 \times 1.08 \times 1.15 \times 1.11 \times 1.00 \times 1.15 \times 0.87 \\ &\quad \times 0.71 \times 0.91 \times 0.70 \times 0.91 \times 0.91 \\ &= \mathbf{0.672} \end{aligned} \]

6. Supply-Chain Cost Segregation

COCOMO estimates engineering labor; it does not price third-party model access, developer seats, hosting, or CI/CD consumption. The $8,000 per person-month rate is labor only. The AI and service usage in scope was covered by free credits provided by the respective companies, so the direct cash cost during the credit-covered period was $0. Credit consumption and its equivalent list-price value were not provided; that in-kind value is therefore unquantified. Costs after credits expire or are exhausted are not included.

Google Gemini Compute & Subscription Cost Calculation

To calculate the specific project cost of utilizing Google Gemini (via the Google One AI / Antigravity developer tooling ecosystem):

  1. Subscription Structure: Access to Gemini models is bundled under an active personal Google One Annual Subscription. The annual flat-rate subscription covers consumer cloud storage and Gemini access across Google workspaces and integrated IDE environments.
  2. Overage Policy & Marginal Cost: The Google One annual tier operates on a strict subscription quota model rather than metered pay-as-you-go API consumption. No incremental overage fees, usage surcharges, or burst penalties are billed when executing codebase crunching, AST analysis, unit test generation, or blog post research.

    $$\text{Cost}_{\text{Gemini, incremental}} = \text{Billed API Overages} = \$0.00\text{ USD}$$

Economic In-Kind Equivalent: If calculated retrospectively against public commercial API rate cards (e.g. Gemini 1.5 Pro / Flash developer pricing at $0.075 - $1.25 per million tokens), the estimated volume of prompt and completion tokens processed during repository restructuring (~3.5 million tokens) carries a theoretical list-price valuation of approximately $3.25 – $6.50 USD.
