## 2. Clinical Target: Asthma & Airway Hyper-Reactivity

### A. Pathophysiology of Asthma & Bronchospasm
Asthma is a chronic inflammatory disorder of the tracheobronchial tree characterized by episodic airflow obstruction:

1. **Bronchoconstriction:** Smooth muscle rings surrounding the bronchioles contract spastically in response to environmental allergens, cold air, exercise, or emotional stress.
2. **Chronic Airway Inflammation:** Infiltration of eosinophils, mast cells, and Th2 lymphocytes causes mucosal swelling, edema, and microvascular leakage.
3. **Mucus Hyper-Secretion:** Hypertrophied goblet cells produce thick, tenacious mucus that plugs narrowed airways, causing coughing, wheezing, and chest tightness.

* **Mental Relief from Respiratory Anxiety:** Calms the nervous tension and fear that accompany restricted breathing, allowing the chest wall and intercostal muscles to relax.
