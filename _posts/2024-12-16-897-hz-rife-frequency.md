- **Macrophage Hijacking & The LCV**: Upon inhalation into the distal lung parenchyma, *L. pneumophila* is phagocytosed by alveolar macrophages via coiling phagocytosis. Inside the LCV, the bacterium multiplies exponentially until the host cell lyses, releasing progeny to infect adjacent macrophages and causing extensive alveolar consolidation, micro-abscesses, and impaired pulmonary gas exchange.
