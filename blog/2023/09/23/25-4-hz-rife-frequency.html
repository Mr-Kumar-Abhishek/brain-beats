A. The Pathophysiology of Sciatic Nerve Entrapment

The sciatic nerve is the largest and longest single nerve in the human body, branching from the lumbosacral plexus through the pelvic notch and down into the feet:

  1. Double-Crush Phenomenon: Sciatica frequently involves compression at two distinct points: central disc herniation / foraminal stenosis at L4–S1, and peripheral muscular impingement beneath a spastic piriformis muscle.
  2. Neuropathic Symptoms: Chronic entrapment causes agonizing pain radiating down the gluteus, hamstring, lateral calf, and foot, accompanied by foot numbness and motor weakness.
