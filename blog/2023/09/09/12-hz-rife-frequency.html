  1. Depressed Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR): Impaired thyroid hormone availability results in cold intolerance, chronic fatigue, fluid retention, and weight gain.
  2. Cognitive Clouding (“Thyroid Brain Fog”): Reduced $T_3$ action in the frontal cortex and hippocampus leads to impaired executive function, memory retrieval delays, and low mood.
  3. 12 Hz Bio-Acoustic Role: 12 Hz provides a non-invasive rhythmic cue to stimulate cellular energy expenditure, promote alert cognitive pacing, and encourage balanced glandular rhythmicity.
