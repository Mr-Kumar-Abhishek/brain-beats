2. Clinical Target: Hyperparathyroidism & Calcium Balance

A. Understanding Hyperparathyroidism & Mineral Homeostasis

The four tiny parathyroid glands, located behind the thyroid in the neck, are the body’s primary regulators of blood calcium and phosphate levels via the secretion of Parathyroid Hormone (PTH):

  1. Pathophysiology of Hyperparathyroidism: Over-activity of the parathyroid glands causes excess PTH secretion, which stimulates osteoclastic bone resorption, increases renal calcium reabsorption, and triggers elevated active vitamin D ($1,25(\text{OH})_2\text{D}$) synthesis.
  2. Clinical Manifestations: Elevated blood calcium (Hypercalcemia) can lead to the classic multi-system symptoms:
    • Bones & Joints: Bone demineralization, osteopenia, deep skeletal aching, and stiffness.
    • Renal System: Increased calcium filtering, kidney stress, and stone formation.
    • Digestive System: Gastric hyperacidity, abdominal cramps, and constipation.
    • Neurological & Mental (“Psychiatric Moans”): Neuromuscular irritability, chronic fatigue, anxiety, depression, brain fog, and severe sleep disturbances.

  • Alleviate Psychological & Cognitive Distress: Relieves the mental clouding, mood swings, and anxiety that frequently accompany parathyroid dysregulation.
  • Glandular Autonomic Support: Enhances parasympathetic blood flow to cervical and endocrine tissue beds, fostering a calmer cellular microenvironment.
  • Splenic Synergy: In multi-frequency protocols, 4.6 Hz is also paired with 147 Hz for spleen support, aiding immune balance and systemic filtration.
