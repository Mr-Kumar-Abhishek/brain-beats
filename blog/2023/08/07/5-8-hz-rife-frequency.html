2. Clinical Target: Tendomyopathy & Musculoskeletal Healing

A. Understanding Tendomyopathy & Myofascial Strain

Tendomyopathy involves chronic pain and functional impairment at the junction between muscles, tendons, and bony attachments (entheses):

  1. Avascular Tendon Entheses: Tendon-bone junctions possess low capillary density, making them prone to slow healing, chronic micro-tears, and persistent inflammation.
  2. Myofascial Trigger Points: Sustained muscle contraction creates localized ischemic knots with elevated concentrations of pain-causing algogens (substance P, bradykinin, lactic acid).
  3. Loss of Elasticity: Chronic strain causes collagen disorganization and stiffness in muscle fascial sheaths.

B. Therapeutic Support Mechanisms of 5.8 Hz

  • Microcirculatory Stimulation: Resonant low-frequency acoustic vibrations encourage nitric oxide release and capillary perfusion in tendon entheses, accelerating tissue repair.
  • Trigger Point Deactivation: Helps disengage locked actin-myosin cross-bridges in tense muscle fibers, relieving deep aching and stiffness.
  • Reduction of Muscle Spasms: Restores natural muscle tone, allowing freer range of motion in the neck, shoulders, back, and limbs.

3. Neurological Target: Vertigo, Headaches & Head Injury Recovery

A. Vestibular Balance & Vertigo Relief

Vertigo arises when mismatched signals occur between the inner ear’s vestibular apparatus, visual cues, and deep cervical neck proprioceptors:

  • Cervicogenic Dizziness: Hypertonic suboccipital muscles transmit erroneous proprioceptive data to the brainstem. 5.8 Hz relaxes suboccipital tension, restoring harmonious neck-eye-vestibular coordination.
  • Central Vestibular Calming: Soothes hyper-excitable vestibular nuclei, reducing room-spinning sensations, nausea, and motion intolerance.

B. Headaches & Post-Head Injury Recovery

  • Vascular & Tension Headaches: Eases pericranial muscular tension and supports autonomic vascular stability to relieve headaches of unknown or mixed origin.
  • Concussion & Head Trauma Follow-up: Provides non-stimulating acoustic entrainment that facilitates restful neurological downtime, mitigating post-concussive cognitive fatigue.
