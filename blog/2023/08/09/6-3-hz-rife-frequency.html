  1. Fluid Equilibrium: Healthy tissues constantly exchange fluid between arterial capillaries and the interstitial space. Approximately 10% of this fluid, along with large protein molecules, cellular debris, and inflammatory cytokines, must be returned to the bloodstream via the lymphatic vessels.

  3. Tissue Toxicity & Pain: Stagnant interstitial fluid creates localized hypoxia, compresses sensory nerve endings, and traps inflammatory algogens, causing throbbing aches and heaviness.
