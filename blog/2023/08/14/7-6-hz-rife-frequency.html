2. Clinical Target: Backache, Lumbago & Spinal Tension

A. The Pathophysiology of Chronic Back Spasms & Pain

Backache is among the leading causes of physical disability worldwide, characterized by self-reinforcing mechanical and neurological loops:

  1. Mechanical Stress & Disc Strain: Poor posture, prolonged sitting, heavy lifting, or degenerative disc changes create uneven mechanical loading across the vertebrae and facet joints.
  2. Reactive Paraspinal Splinting: In response to mechanical irritation, deep postural muscles (multifidus, erector spinae, quadratus lumborum, and psoas) contract involuntarily to “splint” and protect the spine.
  3. Ischemia & Central Sensitization: Sustained contraction restricts local capillary blood flow, accumulating acidic metabolic byproducts (lactic acid, substance P, bradykinin). These chemical algogens continuously stimulate pain receptors, creating chronic hypersensitivity in the central nervous system.

B. Therapeutic Support Mechanisms of 7.6 Hz

  • Melting Deep Myofascial Splinting: 7.6 Hz acoustic vibrations resonate through connective tissue sheaths, encouraging hypertonic muscle fibers to release their grip.
  • Promoting Intervertebral Disc Hydration: Reducing surrounding muscular compression allows nutrient-rich extracellular fluid to diffuse into spinal discs and articular cartilage.
  • Elevating Pain Tolerance: Acoustic entrainment releases central endorphins and restores calmness, reducing anxiety and distress associated with chronic lumbar stiffness.
