2. Clinical Targets: Bronchitis, Airway Spasms & Pulmonary Recovery

A. The Pathophysiology of Bronchitis

Bronchitis is an inflammatory disorder of the lower respiratory tract characterized by irritation and narrowing of the bronchial airways:

  1. Mucosal Congestion & Edema: Viral, bacterial, or environmental irritants trigger inflammation across the bronchial mucosa, stimulating goblet cells to produce copious, viscous mucus.
  2. Reactive Bronchospasm: Hyper-responsive sensory C-fibers in the airway lining trigger reflexive contraction of circular smooth muscle bands, causing wheezing, shortness of breath, and paroxysmal coughing.
  3. 9.35 Hz Bio-Resonant Support: Gentle acoustic resonance at 9.35 Hz fosters parasympathetic bronchodilation, eases smooth muscle hyper-tonicity, and promotes fluid-mucus mobilization without exhausting sensitive lung tissues.
