6. Safety & Clinical Considerations

[!IMPORTANT] Emergency Medical Evaluation for Acute Facial Droop: Sudden onset of facial weakness or drooping requires immediate emergency medical evaluation to rule out acute stroke, transient ischemic attack (TIA), or Lyme neuroborreliosis. Acute Bell’s Palsy benefits significantly from early medical intervention (corticosteroids and antivirals within 72 hours). Sound therapy provides complementary, non-invasive bio-acoustic support.

[!TIP] Eye Protection Care: Because lagophthalmos (inability to close the eyelid fully) often accompanies Bell’s Palsy, ensure frequent application of preservative-free lubricating artificial tears and use an eye patch during sleep to protect the cornea.
