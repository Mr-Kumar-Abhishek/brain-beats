6. Safety & Clinical Considerations

[!IMPORTANT] Rheumatological Medical Care for Systemic Lupus Erythematosus: SLE is a chronic, multi-system autoimmune disease that can affect vital organs (lupus nephritis, central nervous system lupus, pleuropericarditis, cytopenias). Patients must remain under the continuous clinical care of a board-certified rheumatologist, with routine laboratory monitoring (serum creatinine, urinalysis, complement C3/C4 levels, anti-dsDNA titers) and adherence to prescribed immunosuppressive/biologic pharmacotherapies (hydroxychloroquine, mycophenolate, belimumab). Sound therapy serves as an adjunctive supportive wellness modality.

[!TIP] Lupus Care Synergies: Practice strict UV sun protection, adopt an anti-inflammatory Mediterranean or autoimmune protocol diet, and supplement with vitamin D3, omega-3 fish oils, and curcumin.
