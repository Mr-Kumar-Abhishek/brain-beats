  • Tinnitus Tone Quenching & Auditory Cortex Integration: Calms aberrant spontaneous hyperactivity in the dorsal cochlear nucleus and primary auditory cortex, reducing low-frequency roaring subjective tinnitus and relieving aural fullness.
