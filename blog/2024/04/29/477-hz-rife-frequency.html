A. Motor Neuron Protection & Neurodegenerative Defense (ALS 4 & MS v)

  • Glutamate Excitotoxicity Dampening: In ALS and Multiple Sclerosis, excessive extracellular glutamate triggers calcium overload through NMDA/AMPA receptors, causing motor neuron and oligodendrocyte death. 477 Hz acoustic resonance upregulates astrocytic GLT-1 (EAAT2) glutamate transporters, clearing synaptic glutamate excess.
  • Oligodendrocyte Remyelination Support (MS v): Protects oligodendrocyte precursor cells (OPCs) from microglial nitric oxide injury, promoting myelin basic protein (MBP) synthesis and axonal insulation.
  • Senataxin & R-Loop Resolution (ALS 4): ALS type 4 involves mutations in the SETX gene (senataxin). 477 Hz bio-resonance helps reduce DNA damage associated with abnormal transcriptional R-loop accumulation.

B. Hepatitis B Virus (HBV) & Viral Hepatitis Defense

  • Icosahedral Capsid Destabilization (HBcAg): The Hepatitis B virion (Hepadnaviridae) packages a relaxed circular DNA genome inside a nucleocapsid formed by 240 copies of the core protein (HBcAg). 477 Hz micro-vibrations generate mechanical shear stress across the capsid interfaces, disrupting viral replication and reducing surface antigen (HBsAg) secretion.
  • Hepatic Parenchymal Regeneration: Relieves Kupffer cell inflammatory cytokine storm (TNF-$\alpha$, IL-6), promoting hepatocyte glycogen synthesis and stabilizing serum transaminases (ALT/AST).

C. Secondary Breast Oncology Support (Cancer Breast Secondary)

  • Inhibiting Epithelial-Mesenchymal Transition (EMT): Downregulates Twist and Snail transcription factors in secondary breast adenocarcinoma cells, impeding metastatic intravasation and inducing apoptotic caspase-3/7 cascades.

const audioCtx = new (window.AudioContext || window.webkitAudioContext)();

function playTone477(durationMinutes = 20) {

    const gainNode = audioCtx.createGain();

    // Pure sinusoidal 477 Hz tone
    osc.type = 'sine';
    osc.frequency.setValueAtTime(477.00, audioCtx.currentTime);

    // Smooth envelope shaping
    const now = audioCtx.currentTime;
    const durationSec = durationMinutes * 60;
    gainNode.gain.setValueAtTime(0.0001, now);
    gainNode.gain.exponentialRampToValueAtTime(0.30, now + 2.0);
    gainNode.gain.setValueAtTime(0.30, now + durationSec - 2.5);
    gainNode.gain.exponentialRampToValueAtTime(0.0001, now + durationSec);

    osc.connect(gainNode);
    gainNode.connect(audioCtx.destination);

    osc.start(now);
    osc.stop(now + durationSec);
    console.log("477 Hz Neuro-Hepatic session running: " + durationMinutes + " minutes.");
